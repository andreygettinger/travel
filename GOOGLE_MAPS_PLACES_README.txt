GOOGLE MAPS PLACES IMPORT

Your saved Google Maps places have been imported into country HTML pages.
Open index.html and click a green country to see stars. Hover over a star to see its name, click a star to open the original Google Maps link.

places.csv is the editable master list with country, name, latitude, longitude and Google Maps URL.
Coordinates were manually approximated from known place centers, NOT extracted from Google URLs or verified against Google Maps. Check marker accuracy, especially for obscure landmarks, small villages and similarly named places.

The source contained two Magnolia entries for the same area; one marker was used.
Austria, Slovakia and Czechia were added to the homepage because they appear in the supplied list.
No photographs were deleted. To update photo galleries, run the photo updater script if needed.
Maps load geographic boundary data from jsDelivr, so an internet connection is needed.
